🎙️ Natural Neural Voices (on-device) — what's new

BlahBlah now has real, human-sounding neural voices that run entirely on your phone — including ICELANDIC, which iOS doesn't offer at all. They're free, downloaded on demand, and work fully offline once installed.

HOW TO TURN IT ON
1. Profile tab → Settings → "Natural voices (on-device) ✨"
2. Tap "Use natural voices"
3. Download the language(s) you want (German, Spanish, Icelandic, Chinese, Thai) — each is a one-time download
4. Go do a lesson or tap any 🔊 — it now uses the natural voice

WHAT TO TEST
• Download Icelandic and a couple others; confirm the voice sounds natural (not robotic).
• Switch languages and make sure the right voice plays (no stale voice).
• Toggle "Use natural voices" off → it falls back to the system voice.
• Tap 🔊 in lessons, the word bank, and tap-to-translate hints.

Note: if a language hasn't been downloaded (or while a download is in progress), that language keeps using the system voice automatically.

Everything from the last build is still here too: tap-to-translate, crowns, daily quests, streak freeze, achievements.
