🗣️ Auto natural voices · 🔊 Softer sounds · ⌨️ Review fixes · 📚 More vocabulary

• Natural voices: now ON by default and the voice for your course downloads
  automatically in the background — no trip to Settings. You'll hear the system
  voice until it finishes, then it upgrades to the natural one on its own.
• Sounds: correct / complete / wrong are now soft, multi-voice instrument tones
  with a light reverb tail — warm and roomy, not harsh or thin.
• Review: the Check/Continue button rides above the keyboard, the return key
  submits, the box clears between cards, and "turn off typing" now makes Review
  all tap-to-choose too.
• Vocabulary: German, Spanish, French and Italian each gained a new "Most common
  words" section built from open data (high-frequency words + dictionary
  translations). Hundreds of new words per language, validated before shipping.
• Behind the scenes: the build pipeline now also produces Android releases.

WHAT TO TEST
• Open a course → the natural voice should auto-download (watch Settings →
  Natural voices for live progress); speech upgrades automatically when ready.
• Answer correct/wrong, finish a lesson → sounds should be soft & full.
• Review with typing on: type, press return → submits & clears next card; the
  button stays above the keyboard. With typing off → only tap-to-choose.
• Open German/Spanish/French/Italian → scroll to the new "Most common words"
  section near the end and try a few lessons. (These are dictionary-sourced, so
  flag any odd translation — the hand-authored early units remain the polished core.)
